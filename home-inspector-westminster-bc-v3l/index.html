Serving New Westminster

What We Look For
in New Westminster Homes.

New Westminster is BC's oldest incorporated city, and Queens Park in particular has one of the highest concentrations of heritage character homes in the region — many built with balloon framing and knob-and-tube wiring that's still live in some houses today.

The city's hilly terrain means older retaining walls and drainage systems come up often, especially on the streets climbing away from the Fraser River toward Uptown.

Along the Quayside and Downtown waterfront, the housing stock shifts almost entirely to condo towers, where our focus moves to building envelope condition, common-area systems, and strata financials worth flagging for further review.
